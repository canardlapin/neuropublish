Hybrid — the three open questions
docs/design/README.md left these for the design pass. Each is answered below with the reason and what it costs. The rule they all serve: the workspace states what is true of the data and never implies coverage it does not have. Chips mark which answers are changes to what ships today. The single-hemisphere examples are synthetic — in the reference bundle every surface measure carries both hemispheres.
1 · Where does the link state live?
A · Badge in the pane, distance in the status bar chosen a change

The badge names which vertex, beside the surface it belongs to; the status bar keeps only the distance, with the values it qualifies. Costs a little duplication of the number — and it is a change: today the bar carries the whole sentence.
B · Status bar only built today
no badge over the canvas
t · L vertex4.51 surfacelinked to vertex 417, 0.8 mm
What ships today. Nothing covers the mesh, and there is one place to look. But with panes stacked under 900 px the status bar is far from the surface, and a per-pane fact stops being per-pane.
Keep the badge. It is the only element that says which surface is linked, and when the pick comes from the surface the pane badges swap sides — the surface badge is suppressed and the volume pane reads “cursor from surface vertex 417”. That per-pane pairing has no home in a shared bar.
